The world remembers what you leave behind.

The road is buried in ash. The shrines are not as empty as they look. The people who remain have their own loyalties, fears, and reasons to remember you.

The Ashen Chronicle is a dark-fantasy RPG about dangerous journeys, hard-won trust, and a world that does not have to forget you when your character dies.

Get the latest playable build · Discover the game · View the source and follow development

Why enter the Ashen Crown?

Your death is not the end of the story

In many games, a dead character means a reset. Here, death can leave something behind. Deeds, remains, and changes to the world can persist, giving a later character a history to inherit—or letting you choose a fresh beginning.

The world has a memory

People and factions are more than names in a menu. Their memories, reactions, and opinions can change with what you do. Keep track of what happened, earn trust, make enemies, and see how one journey leaves its mark.

There is always another road worth fearing

Travel through a bleak frontier of abandoned markets, black-rooted ravines, old shrines, and a chapel drowned beneath dark water. Take on quests, face turn-based encounters, and find out what waits beyond the next stretch of road.

Quiet horror, strange places, and stories told in the details

A bell rings where no one is standing. A familiar road seems to remember you. Deeper places hold the remains of a caravan, a broken stage, a library of repeating verses, and a tower where sound seems to disappear.

A work in progress, built in the open

The Ashen Chronicle is actively developed and playable, but it is not a finished, content-complete game. Its presentation is still text-led, with simple ASCII scene art; visual polish and a full music-and-sound experience remain part of the work ahead.

The latest public build and platform downloads are on GitHub Releases. Newer work may be available in development before it reaches a release.
